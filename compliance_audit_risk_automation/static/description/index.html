Compliance, Audit & Risk Automation

AI-powered compliance, audit, and risk management for Odoo 18

Overview

The Compliance, Audit & Risk Automation module provides a unified platform for managing audit engagements, compliance frameworks, enterprise risk registers, control testing, and policy lifecycle management. Designed for productivity teams that need structured governance with AI-assisted insights.

Key Features

  • Audit Engagement Management Tracking — Plan, execute, review, and complete internal, external, and regulatory audits with full status tracking.
  • Compliance Framework Tracking SOX / GDPR / HIPAA / ISO 27001 / PCI DSS — Monitor compliance scores and assessment schedules across multiple frameworks.
  • Risk Register Scoring — Track financial, operational, strategic, compliance, and cyber risks with likelihood-impact scoring and AI mitigation prioritization.
  • Control Testing Automation — Run automated, manual, or sample-based control tests with pass/fail/exception results and AI automation potential analysis.
  • Policy Management Lifecycle — Manage policy versions from draft to active to retired with AI compliance gap detection.

AI-Assisted Insights

Each model includes dedicated AI fields — risk assessments, mitigation priorities, automation potential, and compliance gap analysis — enabling teams to leverage artificial intelligence for smarter governance decisions.
